# Stage 1 — Comprehension and orientation

## What this document is

A moderated usability-test transcript (Fireflies recording "Internal feedback: Time logged," 2026-07-06) of an unreleased Hubstaff prototype called "Time Logged" — a redesign of the Timesheets/View-and-Edit area that adds (a) a manager multi-member view (see time for a whole team at once, instead of one member at a time) and (b) batch add-time (add multiple time entries across multiple team members in one action, instead of one member/one day at a time). The feature was briefly rolled out to ~10% of orgs, generated enough negative feedback that it was rolled back, and this session is part of a post-rollback research effort to re-test before a future release.
